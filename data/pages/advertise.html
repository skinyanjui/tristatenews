Put your business in front of people who come to Tri-State News for local jobs, deals, events and classifieds.

How our ads work

  • Ads appear on the Jobs, Deals, Events and Classifieds boards and in the News feed, never directly next to a Police & courts item. There are never ads on Obituaries.
  • Ads aren’t targeted. Everyone who opens a board sees the same ads, taking equal turns through the day. We don’t use cookies or anything about the reader to pick an ad.
  • Every ad is labeled “Sponsored,” and a Featured post is labeled “Featured · Paid.”
  • An ad is one still image with a short headline and text, or words alone, in standard sizes (below). You can also send a finished banner for a size. No pop-ups, video, sound or ads that follow you down the page.
  • A person reviews every ad before it runs.
  • Ads never change what news we show or how we report it.

Ad sizes and where they run

We use standard ad sizes, so a banner you already have may fit. Each ad is built from your picture, headline, short text and button, laid out for the size; or send a finished banner for a size. These previews use a made-up business.
